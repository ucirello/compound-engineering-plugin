# Native peer recovery

Read on a failed start or terminal collection without a usable peer artifact. Keep target, provider, requested model/effort, exact captured base/head range, persona, read-only trust boundary, and shared deadline fixed. A same-route retry is allowed only for a new plausibly recoverable infrastructure failure while time remains; stop on repeated failure or deadline. Never retry actual permission denials, forbidden model arguments, or nesting limits through another harness. Classify those separately from missing native tools, unavailable routes, overload, transport failure, and malformed output.

The native OpenCode dispatch/receipt contract is in `cross-model-review.md`; deleted bridge scripts, acpx, and other harness CLIs are not fallback routes. Configuration and allowlists remain authoritative. Never silently replace an explicit model/provider. A changed recipient requires fresh resolution and disclosure. Preserve receipt evidence and partial diagnostics under the local `.tmp/` run directory.

For an automatically chosen (not configured or user-fixed) unavailable/obsolete model, allow at most one advertised same-family replacement on the same eligible target, recording the rejection and new requested identity before sending. For a never-ran automatically selected recipient, at most one other eligible allowlisted different-family recipient may be resolved and disclosed within the original deadline. A configured/user-fixed route is never silently replaced. A same-route overload retry keeps scope, recipient, model, and deadline fixed and is bounded to one attempt. Otherwise restore the uncovered local lens as below; do not consume retries on permission or nesting failures.

If the peer did not run or yielded no usable review, dispatch one local `adversarial-reviewer` through `dispatch-reviewers.md` when allowed and the lens is still uncovered. Suitable inherited-model reviewers are allowed when delegation is permitted but model overrides are not. Do not claim same-model review as cross-model corroboration. Record `adversarial lens: in-process fallback (cross-model peer not run: <reason>)` and `peer.outcome: in-process-fallback` when applicable. A required different-model pass remains incomplete. If fallback cannot run, Coverage says the adversarial lens did not run; focused review's verdict is Not ready. Full review may retain useful findings but must disclose its incomplete coverage.

At fold-in, distinguish never-started from failed/timeout and from ran-with-no-usable-output; do not turn a failed attempt into a clean skip. A usable empty findings artifact needs no fallback. Never launch a fallback alongside a usable peer on the same brief, nor duplicate a local fallback already started after failed launch. Cancel/reap run-owned outstanding native work through its supported lifecycle, retain evidence, and do not abandon tasks on return.
